Ilias Dimitriadis, Postdoctoral Researcher at Datalab and co-founder of the spin-off company exanta, gave an insightful interview to journalist Alexandra Gouta, discussing his research and the innovative approaches developed by Datalab and exanta in the fields of Large Language Models (LLMs) and Artificial Intelligence.

During the interview on [Homo sAIence](https://www.voria.gr/article/tag/homo-saience), the vidcast of [voria.gr](https://www.voria.gr/), Ilias Dimitriadis explains, through accessible and practical examples, the concept of “emergent capabilities” in modern AI models and how practices such as jailbreaking arise.

The discussion also explores the risks and challenges associated with the rapid development of Large Language Models, highlighting the importance of understanding both their capabilities and their limitations as these technologies continue to evolve.
