Check out the latest episode from "AUTh Innovation Stories", an initiative by the Technology Transfer Office of the Aristotle University of Thessaloniki (AUTH). In the 8th episode of the series, three AUTh spinoffs operating in the fields of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Geoinformatics, are highlighted.
Ilias Dimitriadis (exanta co-founder), shortly presents how exanta connects scientific excellence with multiple market domains in the fields of AI and data analytics, providing fair and trustworthy solutions (video in greek language).

## Few words regarding "AUTh Innovation Stories" series

The series aims to showcase scientific excellence, interdisciplinary research, and the innovative applications developed within our Institution.
This initiative is part of the "AUTh Excellence Strategy" project, funded by the National Recovery and Resilience Plan "Greece 2.0". It aligns with the University’s extroversion strategy, bridging the gap between academic research and society.
